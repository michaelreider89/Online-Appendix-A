Recognition-Primed Decision (RPD) Model
(Klein, 1998)
Mid-Range Theory·FCS 3.82		 A specific cognitive model describing how experts make decisions by recognizing a situation as belonging to a familiar category, identifying the first workable course of action it implies, mentally simulating it, and executing or revising, without comparing multiple options.

  • The recognize-then-simulate sequence gives concrete structure for protocol and training design
  • Validated empirically across firefighting, military command, and emergency medicine

  • Underspecifies how recognition failures (misidentifying a situation) are detected and corrected
